# Demo footage: shot list

Preferred source: footage the builder records. It is fully rights-cleared and authentic. Record only where filming is allowed, and keep people, faces, vehicle plates and signage with names out of frame: the free-credit Meta API account may use uploaded content for product improvement (see CLAUDE.md §3).

## Camera settings
- Phone, rear camera, **1080p at 30 fps** (4K isn't needed and costs upload time). Landscape. Lock exposure and focus if the phone allows, to avoid pumping on shiny metal.
- Hold at chest height, like a technician on a walkdown. Slow, steady walking pace, about half normal speed. No fast pans: SAM tracks better and frames stay sharp.
- Daylight or even overcast light. Avoid backlight from low sun.
- 20–40 s per clip. Start and end on a still frame of 2 s.

## Clips (aim for 3–5)

| # | Subject | What should be in frame | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Rusty railing or handrail run | 10–20 m of railing with patchy surface rust, flaking paint, some clean painted sections | Main "corrosion found" clip; the mix of clean and rusty tests verification |
| 2 | Exposed pipe run (water, gas or HVAC pipes on a building wall or in a plant room you may film) | Pipes, flanges, brackets; any rust at supports and joints | Closest to an industrial pipeline walkdown; supports are a classic corrosion spot |
| 3 | Valve or hydrant close-ups | Valve bodies, bolts, hydrants, standpipes; slow circle around 1–2 of them | Asset-level detail; gives the register clear asset identities |
| 4 | Tank or large painted vessel (water tank, storage tank, large metal container) | Painted steel surface, weld seams, base or anchor bolts | Tank asset type; the base and bolts often corrode |
| 5 | **Clean negatives** | Freshly painted metal, stainless steel, galvanised pipe, *non-rust* stains (water marks, dirt, shadows, orange paint, leaves) | The verifier's main job is rejecting false positives. This clip should produce candidates that Muse Spark rejects |

## Per-clip notes to record (for the register and README)
- Location type (e.g. "campus plant room"), not an exact address.
- Date recorded and device.
- What the builder can see by eye: rough count of rusty spots, any wet or leak stains. This gives a rough ground truth for the demo.

## Backup: CC-licensed industrial footage
Use only if own footage is unusable. Each clip must have an explicit CC0 / CC BY / CC BY-SA licence on its own page, with the author and URL recorded in the README.
- Wikimedia Commons: search "pipeline corrosion", "rusty pipe", "oil refinery pipes" with the Video filter. Licence is shown per file.
- Pexels and Pixabay have their own licences (free to use, not CC). They are acceptable for a demo but must be named accurately. Note that the Pexels/Pixabay licence forbids reselling unmodified copies.
- Not used: YouTube clips (no rights), stock sites with watermarks, any footage from a real operator's facility without written permission.
