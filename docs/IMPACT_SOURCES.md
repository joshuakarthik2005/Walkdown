# Impact sources

Every figure used in the README, UI or video must appear in the **Verified** table with a primary source. Anything in **Candidates** may not be used until it moves to Verified. Figures without a source are left out.

"Primary" means the organisation that produced the figure (the study, the regulator, the investigating board), not a news article or vendor page quoting it.
