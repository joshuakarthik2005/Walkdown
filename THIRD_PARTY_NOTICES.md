## Muse Spark Open (Meta) — Muse Model License

**Built with Muse Spark Open.**

Walkdown calls Muse Spark Open (`meta-models/muse-spark-open-1026`, served by Baseten) for its open-weights
("On-prem") mode: the defect verifier and the planning agent. Any Walkdown verifier model trained on Muse Spark
Open outputs (the Muse Glimmer distillation experiment) is a "Derivative" under this licence, so this licence and
its Usage Policy apply to it as well. No model weights, original or fine-tuned, are distributed in this repository.

How Walkdown meets the Usage Policy:

- **Critical infrastructure (Usage Policy 2.4).** Walkdown is decision support for inspection and maintenance
  planning. It never operates or controls plant equipment, and every maintenance action requires approval by a
  qualified person. See "Limitations and safe use" in `README.md`.
- **Disclosure of known dangers (Usage Policy 4).** Measured limitations (missed corrosion, false alarms,
  unvalidated severity grades) are disclosed in `README.md`, in `benchmark/RESULTS.md` and on screen in the UI.

The licence text and Usage Policy below are reproduced verbatim from the model's Hugging Face repository
(`meta-models/Muse-Spark-Open-816B-A42B`, file `LICENSE.md`), as the licence requires.
